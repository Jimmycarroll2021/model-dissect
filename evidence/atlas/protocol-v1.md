## Acceptance

- **A1 Identity and complete accounting:** machine-readable atlas includes exactly the original checkpoint's full key set (expected91 stored tensors, verify at run time), shapes, dtypes, element counts, storage/tied-alias information and no dropped/unexpected keys. Original checkpoint/config/tokenizer hashes and environment/candidate identity bind every run. Mismatch fails rather than silently substituting a model.
- **A2 Roles and statistics:** every tensor has an evidence-labelled role, layer/operation association or explicit unresolved status; finite/nonfinite and zero counts, min/max, mean, standard deviation, Frobenius norm and dimensions relevant to interpretation. State accumulation precision and zero/empty handling. Nonfinite values fail the affected analysis. List ambiguous orientation/ties and separate numerical evidence from source-supplied labels.
- **A3 Bounded structural analysis:** representative layer comparisons, bounded spectral/rank summaries for selected matrices, vocabulary-row geometry, and explicit Q/K/V/O relationships with shape/orientation and grouped-query head mapping. Spectral estimates must name method, deterministic seed/indices, sample/submatrix shape, truncation and approximation limits. Distinguish approximate/sample rank from full-matrix rank; never imply all91 tensors received SVD. Vocabulary results report exact examined IDs, tokenizer pieces, similarity convention and exclusions; no all-vocabulary pairwise allocation. - **A4 Usable delivery:** actual CLI builds JSON and readable Markdown reports from explicit artifact paths with deterministic options; documented tested command and validation command exist. Report includes tensor table, selected numerical comparisons, a readable dataflow explanation, limits and raw evidence pointers. Independent verifier checks complete key coverage, representative independently computed statistics, deterministic sampling, nonfinite/error handling, dimension/head mapping and original identities against the exact candidate.
- **A5 One investigation:** choose one question from atlas evidence; freeze a separate versioned investigation protocol before any new forward. Reuse the verified same-mode CPU FP32 reconstruction only; no cache repair, no claims dependent on unresolved cache/full stability. Execute baseline, no-op, target intervention, norm-matched internal control and restored baseline; save per-case raw results and intervention norms. Demonstrate cleanup after exceptions and exact post-removal restoration. An evaluator owns fresh confirmation inputs and freezes them before candidate/hypothesis freeze; they remain unseen by the investigator until the permitted confirmation stage. A separate leaf repeats the exact frozen candidate and independently recomputes the verdict. No hypothesis reselection, prompt fishing or threshold tuning after confirmation.
- **A6 Final acceptance:** independent verifier binds source/protocol/report/fixture hashes, checks atlas coverage and investigation evidence, reconciles cumulative resource accounting and untouched originals, and saves a verdict. Coordinator records accepted/partial/blocked in TASK_STATE.md from that evidence. Native goal completes only when all required outcomes pass.
