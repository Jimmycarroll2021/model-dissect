The comparator applies the registered elementwise tolerance to matching continuation positions; current evidence does not identify an incorrect index, mask or invalid expected mathematical relationship. Exact candidate/reference same-mode agreement and intrinsic native self-cache failures show a **different property is being measured**, not that the test is malformed. The same real-arithmetic causal transformer can produce slightly different FP32 values across tensor shapes. [PyTorch numerical-accuracy documentation](https://docs.pytorch.org/docs/2.15/notes/numerical_accuracy.html) explicitly does not guarantee bitwise agreement for batched versus sliced computation. That does not say the project's finite tolerance must fail, nor prove no implementation/backend can meet it.

CPU default, strictCPU and nativeCUDA have failed the stability target; canonical per-query attention instead failed required native-prefill equivalence. These are bounded empirical exclusions. They are not a general impossibility theorem. No additional *evidence-supported minimal fix* currently follows from these results: arbitrary thread,cuBLAS,MKL or mixed-precision sweeps would not be a principled next step. A further unchanged-gate numerical research task could investigate per-element feasibility intervals or error-controlled FP32 reductions, but would require a specified hypothesis and independent native-fidelity checks; this audit does not claim it can deliver within the remaining original window.

The most direct route to the original investigation outcome is a transparently versioned scope change separating same-mode reconstruction fidelity from reference cache/full stability, keeping every failed stability receipt and prohibiting any statement that arithmetic was fixed.
